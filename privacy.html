Privacy Policy

StegoVault Zero guarantees absolute cryptographic privacy and zero network communication.

1. Complete Offline Air-Gap

StegoVault Zero has no internet permissions (INTERNET permission is omitted from the manifest). It is technically incapable of initiating network requests, telemetry uploads, or cloud synchronizations.

2. Plausible Deniability Architecture

The application stores zero indicators or metadata tags revealing whether a secondary secret chamber exists. Cryptographic payload dispersion ensures carrier files contain no detectable markers.

3. Zero Third-Party Tracking

StegoVault Zero contains no advertising SDKs, no behavioral trackers, no analytics frameworks, and no crash-reporting libraries.

4. Local Sandboxed Storage

Carrier media and encrypted payloads reside strictly within your private device sandbox or user-chosen export directories.

5. Inquiries

For questions or verification regarding StegoVault Zero, please visit the official GitHub repository.
